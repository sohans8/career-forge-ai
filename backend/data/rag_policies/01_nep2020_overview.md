# NEP 2020 overview (seed note)

Source: National Education Policy 2020, Ministry of Education, Government of India (https://www.education.gov.in).
This is a short seed summary. Drop the official PDF into this folder and re-run the indexer for full text.

- School structure changes from 10+2 to 5+3+3+4 (Foundational, Preparatory, Middle, Secondary stages).
- Secondary stage (Classes 9-12) is multidisciplinary. Students get more freedom to combine subjects across Science, Commerce and Arts.
- Board exams are meant to test core concepts and be less rote-based. Boards are expected to offer more flexibility. Check CBSE/State board circulars for the current scheme.
- Undergraduate degrees can be 3 or 4 years with multiple entry and exit options: certificate after 1 year, diploma after 2 years, bachelor's degree after 3 years, and a 4-year bachelor's with Honours/Research.
- An Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) stores credits earned from recognised institutions so students can transfer or resume studies.
- A common entrance test route (CUET-UG, conducted by NTA) is used by central universities and many participating universities.
- Vocational education is to be integrated from middle school onward, with internships and exposure to skills.
- The policy is implemented by states and institutions at different speeds. Always verify what applies to your state and college.

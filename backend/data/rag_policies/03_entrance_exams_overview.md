# Major Indian entrance exams overview (seed note)

Always confirm dates, syllabus and fees on the official portal. Exam patterns change.

- JEE Main: National Testing Agency (NTA). B.E./B.Tech and B.Arch/B.Planning admissions at NITs, IIITs and CFTIs via JoSAA/CSAB counselling. Portal: https://jeemain.nta.nic.in
- JEE Advanced: Conducted by one of the IITs on rotation. Open to top JEE Main qualifiers. Required for IIT admissions.
- NEET-UG: NTA. Single entrance for MBBS, BDS, AYUSH and BSc Nursing. Counselling by MCC (all-India quota) and states. Portal: https://neet.nta.nic.in
- CUET-UG: NTA. Common entrance for undergraduate programmes at central and participating universities. Portal: https://cuet.nta.nic.in
- MHT-CET: State CET Cell, Maharashtra. Engineering, pharmacy and other admissions in Maharashtra via CAP. Portal: https://cetcell.mahacet.org
- CLAT: Consortium of National Law Universities. Law admissions to NLUs.
- GATE: Conducted by IITs on rotation. Used for M.Tech admissions and some PSU recruitment.
- CA Foundation / Intermediate / Final: ICAI. CS (CSEET and later stages): ICSI.
- NDA and CDS: UPSC, followed by SSB interviews.
- SSC JE and RRB JE: SSC and Railway Recruitment Boards for diploma holders.

If a student asks about a specific year, give the general process and tell them to check the official portal for the current-year schedule, since this note does not carry live dates.

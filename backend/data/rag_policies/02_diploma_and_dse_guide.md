# Diploma (Polytechnic) and Direct Second Year (DSE) guide (seed note)

Sources to verify against: AICTE Approval Process Handbook (https://www.aicte-india.org), State DTE / CET Cell portals, MSBTE (Maharashtra).

## Diploma after 10th
- Engineering diplomas run 3 years (6 semesters) after Class 10. Common branches: Computer, IT, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Electronics and Telecommunication, and newer branches such as AI/ML and Data Science at selected institutes.
- Admission is usually through state centralised admission rounds (CAP) run by the Directorate of Technical Education, based on Class 10 marks.
- Diploma programmes are approved by AICTE and affiliated to a state technical board (for example MSBTE in Maharashtra).

## Direct Second Year (DSE) engineering
- Diploma holders can join the second year of B.E./B.Tech through lateral entry (called Direct Second Year Engineering in Maharashtra, and ECET/LEET or similar in other states).
- Admission is generally merit-based on diploma marks through a state CAP process. The CET Cell runs it in Maharashtra.
- The AICTE handbook sets national eligibility rules for lateral entry. States may add their own minimum marks and seat reservations. Verify the current year's brochure before applying.
- After DSE the degree takes 3 more years (second to fourth year).

## Jobs after diploma
- Government: SSC JE (central), RRB JE (Railways), state PWD, irrigation and electricity board JE posts, and PSU diploma trainee roles. Check each notification for age limits and eligibility.
- Private: technician, junior developer, site supervisor and quality roles. Apprenticeships are available through NATS and NAPS portals.
